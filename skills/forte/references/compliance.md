# Compliance & Certifications

> **"Compliance" has two meanings in Forte.** This page covers **certifications** (HIPAA/BAA, SOC 2).
> Separately, **Compliance Registration** is the console onboarding form
> (`forteplatforms.com/console/compliance`) an account completes to become the merchant of record and
> to send messages to end users. An **APPROVED** registration gates **live payments** (see
> `payments.md`) and **enabling email / phone / password login** (see `auth-and-proxy.md`) — the
> latter also needs a verified SES sending domain (email) and a `COMPLETE` SMS registration (phone).

## HIPAA & BAA
For HIPAA compliance and to request a Business Associate Agreement (BAA), contact Forte support.

## SOC 2
SOC 2 compliance is in progress. Contact Forte support for questions or more information.

For all compliance inquiries, contact Forte support at support@forteplatforms.com.
